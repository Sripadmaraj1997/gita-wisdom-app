Gita Wisdom Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 22, 2026

Gita Wisdom is a Bhagavad Gita reading, journaling, saved verse, and spiritual guidance app. This policy explains how the MVP handles information.

Information We Collect

Gita Wisdom may collect your email address, display name, saved verses, journal entries, owner/admin interpretations, notification preference, and Ask Gita questions when AI features are enabled.

How We Use Information

We use information to sign you in, save your content, deliver daily reminders, provide Ask Gita AI responses, and maintain app security. We do not sell personal information.

Ask Gita AI

When Ask Gita AI is enabled, your question and relevant app context may be sent to an AI provider to generate a response. Do not enter sensitive personal, medical, legal, or financial information. AI responses are for spiritual reflection only.

Storage and Security

The app uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Firestore rules are designed to restrict user-owned content to the signed-in user and owner-only content to the configured owner/admin account.

Your Choices

You can delete journal entries and saved verses in the app, turn daily reminders on or off in Settings, and sign out at any time.

Children

Gita Wisdom is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it if needed.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact pamidip@hotmail.com.
