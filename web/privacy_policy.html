Gita Wisdom Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

Gita Wisdom is a Bhagavad Gita reading, journaling, saved verse, and spiritual guidance app. This policy explains how the local/offline MVP handles information.

Information We Collect

Gita Wisdom stores saved verses, highlights, journal entries, Ask Gita Lite question history, reading preferences, Journey progress, and continue-reading progress locally on your device.

How We Use Information

We use local information to show saved content, restore your reading place, remember Journey progress, keep reader preferences, and provide Ask Gita Lite responses from local Bhagavad Gita data. We do not sell personal information.

Ask Gita Lite

Ask Gita Lite uses local retrieval and local Bhagavad Gita content. The current MVP does not send questions to OpenAI or another AI provider. Do not enter sensitive personal, medical, legal, or financial information. Responses are for spiritual reflection only.

Storage and Security

The current MVP does not require login and does not use Firebase. User-owned content is stored in local device storage using app preferences. Clearing app data or using the in-app clear data action removes local saved content from the device.

Your Choices

You can delete journal entries, saved verses, highlights, Ask Gita history, Journey progress, and reader preferences from Settings using Clear Saved Data.

Children

Gita Wisdom is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it if needed.
